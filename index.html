About Us

SPEED is an agricultural drone service company dedicated to helping farmers adopt modern and efficient farming practices. We specialize in drone-based spraying solutions for fertilizers, pesticides, insecticides, herbicides, Nano Urea, Nano DAP, and other crop nutrients.

Using advanced drone technology, we ensure precise and uniform spraying across fields, reducing labor requirements, saving time, and minimizing the wastage of agricultural inputs. Our services are designed to improve crop health, increase productivity, and support sustainable farming methods.

At SPEED, we believe that technology can play a vital role in the future of agriculture. Our team is committed to providing reliable, safe, and cost-effective drone services that make farming smarter and more efficient. By combining innovation with agricultural expertise, we aim to empower farmers and contribute to the growth of modern agriculture.

We strive to build long-term relationships with farming communities by delivering quality service, professional support, and solutions that meet the evolving needs of the agricultural sector.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To transform modern agriculture through innovative drone technology, enabling farmers to achieve higher productivity and sustainability.

Mission
