Crop monitoring using drones enables farmers to assess crop health, identify pest infestations, detect nutrient deficiencies, and monitor field conditions in real time. This helps improve decision-making and overall farm productivity.

Nano Urea spraying helps improve nitrogen absorption in crops, promotes healthy plant growth, and reduces the need for conventional urea fertilizers. It increases nutrient efficiency while minimizing environmental impact.

Nano DAP provides essential nitrogen and phosphorus nutrients required for root development and crop growth. It helps improve plant vigor and enhances overall yield.

Sagarika is a seaweed-based organic growth promoter that improves plant health, stimulates root development, and enhances resistance to environmental stress conditions.

Drone-based pesticide spraying provides uniform coverage across the field, helping protect crops from harmful pests while reducing chemical wastage and labor costs.

Insecticide spraying helps control insect infestations that can damage crops and reduce productivity. Drone application ensures accurate and efficient coverage even in difficult-to-reach areas.

Herbicide spraying is used to control unwanted weeds that compete with crops for nutrients, water, and sunlight. Precision drone spraying improves effectiveness while reducing excess chemical usage.
